### Reviewed and kept (evidence on production unless noted)

- `.press` (`globals.css:293-301`): scale 0.97, 100 ms in on `:active`, 200 ms `--ease-out` release; `.press-dim` and `.cell` 0 ms in, 200 ms out. Asymmetric the right way round; reduced motion drops the transform.
- Sheet (`bits.tsx:220-370`, `globals.css:361-382`): panel 420 ms `--ease-drawer` in from `translateY(100%)` via `@starting-style`, 300 ms `--ease-out` out; scrim 300 in, 200 out. Measured: `transform 420ms cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` open, `transform 300ms cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` close. Inside the modal budget; gesture reviewed in rounds 11 and 18.
- Toggle knob (`bits.tsx:207`): transform 200 ms `--ease-out`, track colour derived from the drag. Thumb (`thumb.tsx:38`): transform 200 ms `--ease-out`; `width` transitions only when segments differ in width, test-exempt and on a 2 px-high track.
- Disclosure (`bits.tsx:95-98`): chevron 200 ms; `grid-template-rows` 300 ms is the accordion case AUDIT.md allows, with `motion-reduce:transition-none`. Re-measure if a disclosure ever holds a long passage.
- `.img-fade` 300 ms `--ease-out` on a portrait known to be loading only; `.bar-fill` scaleX 300 ms; `.plus-bar` 200 ms; `.scroll-edge` 200 ms fade gated by `data-ready` so the bar never animates on first paint.
- `.tick` draw (420 ms plus 150 ms delay) on booking and calendar connected: rare success moments, the one place the delight budget is spent. Splash `settle` with a 350 ms delay: first run only.
- Searching `word-in` (30 ms stagger, capped at 8) and the step line: a judged moment (round 3).
- Tab hop Home to Recent, measured: one `fade 150ms`, one `transform 200ms` on the tab-bar lens, five `rise 300ms` on Recent rows that come from storage after mount. Nothing on a layout property.
- Reduced motion (`globals.css:754-802`): gentler, not zero (page fade to 100 ms, sheet becomes a 200 ms fade, rises and loops off, toast becomes opacity-only).

Rejected candidates, with the gate question that killed each:

- Tab-bar hops beyond the 150 ms fade and the lens slide (`index.tsx:406-420`). Core navigation, tens of times a day; iOS switches tabs instantly. Never add a push or a stagger here.
- Search submitted with Enter (`Home.tsx:115-119`). Keyboard-initiated. The 0.9 s searching floor is a judged exception (round 3); add nothing to it.
- The Searching overlay's exit (`Narrow.tsx:290`). It leaves with the route, under the page fade; a second fade would double it.
- Saved and Recent empty states (`Tabs.tsx:134-137`). They are pre-hydration server HTML (round 13); an entrance would bring back the blank frame that round removed.
- Clamp "More" (`bits.tsx:62-80`). Expanding `line-clamp` is a layout change; the `rise` on the extra content is the right amount.
- Compare table, profile facts, pager dots, skeleton fills. Data being read, or placeholders: decoration hinders, and the flat skeleton (no sweep) is by design.
- Desktop hover beyond `press-dim`'s opacity. Tens of times a day; already gated by `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.

## Do not animate

- Tab hops, Back, and anything the keyboard drives (focus moves, Enter, Escape closing the sheet): the response is instant; the only motion is the existing page fade.
- Page wrappers (`app/template.tsx:3`): opacity only, never transform; a filled transform pins fixed docks to the page (round 3).
- Text a reader is reading: profiles, Compare, the fact rows, review themes. No stagger, no entrance beyond the page fade (finding 2 removes the ones that exist).
- Skeletons and pre-hydration states: a flat fill that is replaced whole, no shimmer sweep, no fade on replacement (rounds 12 and 13).
- Hover on touch: nothing outside the `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` gate.
- The Toggle track, the sheet scrim and the toast's opacity during a drag: derived from the finger's distance, never on their own clock.
- The Home example rows and faces: tapped tens of times a day; press feedback only.
- The splash leaf: still (`.float` is a no-op and can go).

## Verdict

The system is close to right: five tokens, one curve for UI, exits faster than entrances everywhere except the toast, nothing on a layout property, gestures on transitions, reduced motion gentler rather than off. The corrections are the heart's overshoot (the one rule break), and `rise` running on content that was already on the page, which costs the most visited screen 200 ms of blank title and the prerendered pages a 300 ms entrance they do not need. Highest leverage: finding 1 (one keyframe, one duration), then finding 2 and 3 together (less motion, earlier paint).

Temporary scripts used for the measurements were placed under `qa/.tmp/` and deleted; `qa/edge/animations.mjs` reproduces the frame sampling on every route.
